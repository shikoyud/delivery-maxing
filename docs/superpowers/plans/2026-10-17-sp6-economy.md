# SP6 Economy Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fix the five SP5 playtest items (a whole-city map outside the phone, a food-prep progress bar, a skateboard jump, getting off at any speed, WASD-only vehicle control with mouse look), then build the economy of spec §15 row 6: the Coin shop (fuel, cosmetics), the Token shop (vehicles, Shoes, premium skins, lockout skip) wired to a mock on-chain source, the Wardrobe, and real Garage and Wallet apps.

**Architecture:** Tasks 1–6 change the shared ride sim (drive input becomes throttle and steer, dismount loses its speed limit, the skateboard gets an ollie), the client camera on rides (mouse orbits, the camera swings back behind the vehicle) and the UI (a full-screen `MapScreen` replaces the phone's Map app; the HUD's prep row becomes a bar). Tasks 7–16 add `packages/shared/src/economy/` (shop catalog, USD→GAME pricing with the §6.3 safety limits, the 80/20 split, the `ChainSource` interface), a client `MockChain` that implements `ChainSource` in localStorage with a confirmation delay and dev-panel failure switches, a client `Purchases` controller that turns confirmed transactions into grants (vehicles into the `Garage`, Shoes into the sim's stat modifiers, skins and Coin cosmetics into the Wardrobe), and the Shop, Wardrobe, Garage and Wallet phone apps. Nothing touches Solana: no RPC, no devnet, no keys.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, pnpm workspaces (`@dm/shared`, `@dm/client`, `@dm/tools`), Three.js 0.186, Rapier, Vitest, Playwright (headless Chromium).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-delivery-maxing-design.md`: §2, §3.3 (offers follow the vehicle, switching blocked when orders don't fit), §3.5 (lockout skip), §4 (camera), §4.1 (input layer), §4.2 (mount, reverse, ownership), §4.4 (fuel), §6 (Coins, GAME purchases, USD pricing), §8 (phone and HUD), §11 (purchases take effect after confirmation), §13 (wallet rejects, purchase pending, rate stale), §15 row 6. The SP5 playtest write-up: `/mnt/project-files/knowledge/claude/sp5/sp5-playtest-feedback.md`. This PR updates the spec for playtest items 2, 4, 5 and 6 (shiko's decisions of 2026-10-17); the build PR updates it for the Decisions below once shiko approves them.

**Base:** `main` with SP5 merged (PR #12). File paths and line references are to main.

## Decisions to confirm (defaults this plan uses)

The spec is silent, ambiguous or **Pending** on these. Each is built as written unless shiko says otherwise when approving the plan.

1. **USD prices are placeholders.** Spec §6.2 leaves every USD price Pending. `config/economy.json` ships these, each easy to change: skateboard $2, bicycle $5, motorbike $12, car $25; Shoes tiers 1–5 $1, $2, $3, $4, $5; each premium skin $3; offer-lockout skip $0.50. The mock rate is $0.00024 per GAME, so "$10 ≈ 41,667 GAME".
2. **The mock chain lives in the browser.** `MockChain` keeps a dev wallet (`DEV-WALLET`, shown with a "Mock wallet" badge everywhere GAME appears) in localStorage, starts it with 300,000 GAME (≈ $72 at the mock rate: every vehicle, $44, plus some Shoes and skins), confirms each transaction after 2 s, and records the burned and pool amounts so the split can be checked. The dev panel adds GAME, sets the rate, makes the rate stale and makes the next transaction fail. Wallet sign-in stays SP7.
3. **A new player starts on foot, owning nothing.** Vehicle ownership comes from the mock chain from now on. SP5 saves keep their Coins, points and deliveries, but their vehicles are not carried over: shiko buys them again with the starting GAME, or ticks them on in the dev panel (which now grants and revokes on the mock chain).
4. **Cosmetics are colours and finishes.** Outfit colours (the courier's main material), vehicle paint (all owned rides; the pack car included), bag colours (the boxes the courier carries) and phone cases (the phone's frame), bought with Coins in sets of six per slot. Premium skins (GAME) are three finishes, Gold, Chrome and Neon, each for the outfit and for vehicle paint. Helmets and emotes wait for models and animations (not in this plan).
5. **"Fuel cards" are fuel cans.** A fuel can adds 25% of a tank to the ridden or last-ridden motorbike or car wherever it is parked or ridden, for 1.5 × the pump price of that fuel (motorbike 15 Coins, car 30 Coins). Pumps stay the cheap way to refuel.
6. **The Garage app's Switch picks the vehicle offers are for.** On foot, Switch on an owned vehicle sets the tier offers and capacity follow (today: the last one ridden). It is refused, with "Your orders don't fit on the bicycle", when the carried orders don't fit that tier (§3.3). Mounting a vehicle still switches too.
7. **WASD steering.** On a vehicle W is throttle, S brakes and then reverses (car 6 m/s, bicycle 2, motorbike 2.5; the skateboard only brakes), A and D turn the heading at the vehicle's speed-dependent turn rate, also when stopped (the in-place turn every ride has today). Reversing steers like a real car: A swings the nose right. The SP5 "turn toward back input first, back out while refused" logic is removed with its config keys (`riding.backInputDot`, `riding.turnFirstMaxReverse`, `riding.backOutSeconds`); a wedged bike gets out with A/D or S.
8. **The camera on a vehicle.** The mouse orbits the camera freely around the ride. After 1.5 s without mouse movement, while the ride moves faster than 2 m/s, it eases back behind the heading. Getting on or off never makes the camera jump.
9. **Getting off at any speed.** The vehicle stops and parks where it is at once (the skateboard is picked up), and the courier steps off to the side keeping the ride's horizontal velocity up to the on-foot cap (12 m/s). Still refused when no spot beside the vehicle fits a standing courier ("No room to get off"), and in the air for any ride but the skateboard (nothing is ever parked in the air). The "Slow down to get off" message and `riding.dismountMaxSpeed` are removed.
10. **The skateboard ollie.** Space jumps 0.8 m on the skateboard, keeping its speed, with the on-foot coyote time and jump buffer (100 ms each). The skateboard has no handbrake (S brakes). Space stays the handbrake on the bicycle, motorbike and car.
11. **The map is a screen, not an app.** M opens a full-screen map at any speed and on foot: the whole city fitted to the window, mouse-wheel zoom from 1× to 6× toward the cursor, drag to pan, and a legend (you, orders, offers, hospitals, gas stations, your parked vehicles). M or Esc closes it. While it is open the sim gets only the look (a ride coasts down, as with the phone). The phone loses its Map tab (seven apps); the minimap stays.
12. **The prep bar.** A food order waiting at the kitchen shows a bar in its HUD row filling over `prep.foodSeconds`, labelled "Preparing", then "Ready for pickup" in green. The arrival popup says "Preparing <item>" with "Wait at the counter" and no seconds.
13. **Two SP5 leftovers ride along** because buying vehicles now parks them at bays: `bays.spacing` grows from 4 m to 5.5 m so a car (4.4 m long) fits any slot (the navmeshes are rebuilt), and the pack car model is scaled to its 4.4 m collider (it is 3.3 m).
14. **GAME buys are quoted with 2% slippage.** The buyer's `maxGame` is the quote × 1.02 (config), the on-chain check of §6.3. A rate older than 15 minutes pauses purchases ("Prices updating, purchases paused").
15. **Out of this plan:** per-order GAME accrual, the Claim button and claim history (§9 is Pending; the Wallet app keeps the "arrives with the reward program" note); accepted orders surviving a reload (SP7 persistence); the parapet stays a running jump (SP5 ruling); the Leaderboard stays a stub (SP7).

Expected: the car parks at that hospital's first free slot without overlapping them (5.5 m spacing), with a popup naming the hospital, and Summon can bring it. Test in Task 9.

Expected: the courier is never placed inside geometry (right side or behind is tried, else "No room to get off" and still riding), keeps at most 12 m/s, and lands normally. Test in Task 3.
5. **Driving while looking backwards.** The camera is orbited to face behind the car and W is held. Expected: the car drives along its own heading, not toward the camera; A/D still turn the nose left/right; after 1.5 s without mouse movement the camera swings back behind. Test in Task 1 (sim) and Task 2 (camera).
