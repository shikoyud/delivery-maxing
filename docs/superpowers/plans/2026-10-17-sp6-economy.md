# SP6 Economy Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fix the five SP5 playtest items (a whole-city map outside the phone, a food-prep progress bar, a skateboard jump, getting off at any speed, WASD-only vehicle control with mouse look) and shiko's new pickup deadline (3 minutes to reach the pickup, else the order fails and offers stop for 3 minutes), then build the revision 3 economy of spec §15 row 6 on a browser mock of the chain: the four upgrade lines (Vehicle, Bag, Shoes, Dispatcher) and the Coin shop, the per-order USD-valued GAME payout roll, lock-to-boost, and the Shop, Wardrobe, Garage and Wallet apps.

**Architecture:** Tasks 1–7 change the shared ride sim (drive input becomes throttle and steer, dismount loses its speed limit, the skateboard gets an ollie), the client camera on rides (mouse orbits, the camera swings back behind the vehicle) and the UI (a full-screen `MapScreen` replaces the phone's Map app; the HUD's prep row becomes a bar; `OrderSystem` gains a pickup deadline). Tasks 8–20 add `packages/shared/src/economy/` (the upgrade lines and their tiers, USD→GAME pricing with the §6.3 safety limits, the 80/20 split, the payout roll, the boost multiplier, Bag×vehicle capacity, Dispatcher tuning, the `ChainSource` interface), a client `MockChain` that implements `ChainSource` in localStorage (balance, vault, ownership per line, locks, accrued payouts, delayed confirmation, dev failure switches), a client `Purchases` controller that turns confirmed transactions into grants, the payout roll on every completed order, and the Shop, Wardrobe, Garage and Wallet phone apps. Nothing touches Solana: no RPC, no devnet, no keys. SP8 replaces `MockChain` with the real program behind the same `ChainSource`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, pnpm workspaces (`@dm/shared`, `@dm/client`, `@dm/tools`), Three.js 0.186, Rapier, Vitest, Playwright (headless Chromium).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-delivery-maxing-design.md` revision 3: §2 (rev. 3 rows), §3.3 (Bag capacity and vehicle caps), §3.4 (Dispatcher), §3.5 (lockout skip, the pickup deadline), §3.6 (points, Coins and the GAME payout roll), §4 (camera), §4.1 (input layer), §4.2 (mount, reverse, ownership, caps, ranges, prices), §4.4 (fuel), §6 (Coins, the four lines, USD pricing, lock-to-boost), §8 (phone and HUD), §9 (reward program), §11 (purchases take effect after confirmation), §13 (wallet rejects, purchase pending, rate stale), §15 row 6. ## Decisions to confirm (defaults this plan uses)

The spec leaves these open. Each is built as written unless shiko says otherwise when approving the plan.

1. **The mock chain lives in the browser.** `MockChain` keeps a dev wallet (`DEV-WALLET`, shown with a "Mock wallet" badge everywhere GAME appears) in localStorage, starts it with 600,000 GAME (≈ $144 at the mock rate of $0.00024 per GAME: every tier of every line costs $124), confirms each transaction after 2 s, and records burned and vault amounts. The mock vault starts with $50 of GAME. The dev panel adds GAME, sets the rate, makes the rate stale, fails the next transaction, empties or refills the vault, and advances the lock clock. Wallet sign-in stays SP7.
2. **A new player starts on foot at tier 0 of every line.** SP5 saves keep their Coins, points and deliveries, but vehicles come from the chain now: shiko buys them again with the starting GAME, or ticks them on in the dev panel (which grants on the mock chain).
3. **Payout luck is rolled on the client against the mock**, with a seeded RNG recorded per delivery (`luck`, `quality`, `rate`), as the server will record it in SP7. The mock credits the GAME to the wallet's accrued balance; a mock Claim moves it to the balance at once.
4. **Cosmetics are colours and finishes.** Outfit colours (the courier's main material), vehicle paint (all owned rides; the pack car included), bag colours (the boxes the courier carries) and phone cases (the phone's frame), bought with Coins in sets of six per slot. Premium skins (GAME) are three finishes, Gold, Chrome and Neon, each for the outfit and for vehicle paint. Helmets and emotes wait for models and animations.
5. **"Fuel cards" are fuel cans.** A fuel can adds 25% of a tank to the ridden or last-ridden motorbike or car wherever it is, for 1.5 × the pump price of that fuel (motorbike 15 Coins, car 30 Coins).
6. **The Garage app's Switch picks the vehicle offers are for.** On foot, Switch on an owned vehicle sets the tier offers, capacity and the payout range follow (today: the last one ridden). It is refused, with "Your orders don't fit on the bicycle", when the carried orders don't fit the capacity that tier caps (§3.3). Mounting a vehicle still switches too.
7. **WASD steering.** On a vehicle W is throttle, S brakes and then reverses (car 6 m/s, bicycle 2, motorbike 2.5; the skateboard only brakes), A and D turn the heading at the vehicle's speed-dependent turn rate, also when stopped. Reversing steers like a real car: A swings the nose right. The SP5 "turn toward back input first, back out while refused" logic is removed with its config keys (`riding.backInputDot`, `riding.turnFirstMaxReverse`, `riding.backOutSeconds`).
8. **The camera on a vehicle.** The mouse orbits the camera freely around the ride. After 1.5 s without mouse movement, while the ride moves faster than 2 m/s, it eases back behind the heading. Getting on or off never makes the camera jump.
9. **Getting off at any speed.** The vehicle stops and parks where it is at once (the skateboard is picked up), and the courier steps off to the side keeping the ride's horizontal velocity up to the on-foot cap (12 m/s). Still refused when no spot beside the vehicle fits a standing courier ("No room to get off"), and in the air for any ride but the skateboard. The "Slow down to get off" message and `riding.dismountMaxSpeed` are removed.
10. **The skateboard ollie.** Space jumps 0.8 m on the skateboard, keeping its speed, with the on-foot coyote time and jump buffer. The skateboard has no handbrake (S brakes). Space stays the handbrake on the bicycle, motorbike and car.
11. **The map is a screen, not an app.** M opens a full-screen map at any speed and on foot: the whole city fitted to the window, mouse-wheel zoom from 1× to 6× toward the cursor, drag to pan, and a legend. M or Esc closes it. While it is open the sim gets only the look. The phone loses its Map tab (seven apps); the minimap stays.
12. **The prep bar.** A food order waiting at the kitchen shows a bar in its HUD row filling over `prep.foodSeconds`, labelled "Preparing", then "Ready for pickup" in green. The arrival popup says "Preparing <item>" with "Wait at the counter" and no seconds.
13. **Two SP5 leftovers ride along** because buying vehicles now parks them at bays: `bays.spacing` grows from 4 m to 5.5 m so a car (4.4 m long) fits any slot (the navmeshes are rebuilt), and the pack car model is scaled to its 4.4 m collider (it is 3.3 m).
14. **GAME buys are quoted with 2% slippage.** The buyer's `maxGame` is the quote × 1.02 (config), the on-chain check of §6.3. A rate older than 15 minutes pauses purchases ("Prices updating, purchases paused") and payouts are converted at the last good rate.
15. **Vehicle ownership is a line.** Buying the motorbike gives the skateboard and bicycle too (tiers in order, §4.2 rev. 3); the Garage shows every owned tier. The capacity cap and payout range follow the tier in use or last parked (§3.3).
16. **The Dispatcher's "quality" is two config weights**: at tier t the long band's selection weight is multiplied by `1 + 0.3 t` and every modifier's weight (Express, Fragile) by `1 + 0.2 t`, applied where `generateOffer` rolls the band and modifier. Pickup distance and the 180 s door reuse are unchanged.
17. **Out of this plan:** the Claim transaction and claim history on a real chain (SP8; the mock Claim is instant); accepted orders surviving a reload (SP7 persistence); the parapet stays a running jump (SP5 ruling); the Leaderboard stays a stub (SP7); thieves (SP7).
