# SP5 City v2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the greybox with the 1.2 × 1.2 km terraced bay city of the city map design (levels, slope bands, switchbacks, steep lanes, stairs, districts, landmarks, docks, beach, harbor, ridge), generated deterministically from the asset catalog into `map/city.layout.json`, validated by `pnpm map:check`, rendered with the owner's packs when they are installed and as placeholder boxes when they are not, streamed and LOD-ed at 60 fps; and fix the SP4 playtest findings first (a two-wheeler wedged beside a wall, silent pumps and missing key prompts, the car's nose in alleys, bay vehicles facing a wall).

**Architecture:** The shared package gains the catalog and layout schemas (`world/catalog.ts`, `world/cityLayout.ts`), `layoutToMap` (lots, terrain, roads, connectors and parapets become `Solid`s; alleys, lanes, stairs and paths become `AccessZone`s; doors become `Poi`s), and the SP2 branch's chunk maths and collider pager so only the solids near the player have Rapier colliders. `packages/tools` gains the generator (`city/`), the validator, `assets:check` / `assets:import` / `assets:sheet`, per-tier navmeshes with per-tier cell sizes and a pickup→dropoff distance table, all written by `pnpm map:build` and byte-compared by `pnpm map:check`. The client gains a `CityView` (the SP2 one reshaped: one merged placeholder mesh per block as the far LOD and the fallback, instanced pack models in two near rings loaded lazily per catalog id), code-built terrain and road regions, radial fog, the map debug view, the `?perf=1` flythrough, and `?map=city|greybox`. The SP4 greybox test town stays as `?map=greybox` for the vehicle tests.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, pnpm workspaces (`@dm/shared`, `@dm/client`, `@dm/tools`), Three.js 0.186 (GLTFLoader + MeshoptDecoder, InstancedMesh), Rapier, `recast-navigation` 0.43.1, `@gltf-transform/core|extensions|functions` 4.5.1 and `meshoptimizer` 1.3.0 (the SP2 branch's versions), Node `zlib` and the browser `DecompressionStream` for gzip, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-delivery-maxing-design.md` (revision 2): §2 rows City, Art, Map build; §4.2 Models; §4.3 (access per surface); §4.4 (six gas stations); §5 City and POIs; §12 (packages, `map/`, `assets/`); §13 Vehicle stuck; §14 Map and Client testing; §15 row 5; §16 asset licence, performance risk, open items. City map design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-city-map-design.md` (all sections; §7 and §9 most of all). SP4 findings: `/mnt/project-files/reviews/sp4-playtest/README.md` and `/mnt/project-files/knowledge/claude/sp4-status.md` "Known gaps". SP2 code reused from the closed PR #3 head (`git fetch origin refs/pull/3/head`): `world/chunks.ts`, `world/colliderPager.ts`, `render/city/CityView.ts`, `render/city/lodRings.ts`, `render/city/regions.ts`, `render/radialFog.ts`, `game/perf.ts`, `ui/perfOverlay.ts`, `ui/mapDebug.ts`, `tools/src/city/stableJson.ts`, `tools/src/assets/pipeline.ts`.

## Decisions to confirm (defaults this plan uses)

The spec is silent or ambiguous on these. Each is built as written below unless shiko says otherwise when approving the plan.

1. **How the packs reach the build.** The packs are not in the repository. On 2026-10-15 they were copied from shiko's machine into the project's shared files as `assets/delivery-maxing-packs.zip` (931 `.glb`, 162 MB unpacked, every file the catalog lists and nothing else; see `.notes/inputs.md` there). The build thread unpacks it into its git-ignored `assets/packs/`. GitHub Pages still deploys the placeholder city: letting CI fetch the packs needs a private store and a deploy token, which is a CI change and waits for shiko's go (project rule).
2. **60 fps proof with the real models happens on shiko's machine.** Headless SwiftShader renders at about 1 fps, so the sandbox can only prove draw-call and triangle budgets. Task 14 ends with a checkpoint: shiko runs `?perf=1` locally and pastes the result panel's JSON (as in SP2).
3. **Two-wheelers back up at walking pace.** The bicycle and motorbike get `reverseSpeed` 2 and 2.5 m/s (the skateboard stays 0: an upright capsule is never refused a turn). Holding S while stopped backs the bike out of a corner. This is the fix for the wedged motorbike, together with turning the long way round when the short way is blocked (Task 1). The SP4 plan's "the other vehicles turn around" default is replaced by this.
4. **Steering stays camera-relative** (SP4 Decision 4). The playtest review asked whether A and D should steer the vehicle instead. This plan does not change steering; if shiko wants A/D steering it is a separate task shiko names.
5. **Key prompts** (Task 2): a prompt line at the bottom centre of the HUD, in the style of the popups: "F · Ride the car" (next to a parked owned vehicle, on foot), "F · Get off" (stopped on a ride), "Hold E · Refuel" (stopped on the motorbike or car within pump radius), "Get on to refuel" (on foot within pump radius of a pump, owning a ride with a tank). With 0 Coins at a pump the popup says "No Coins for fuel"; a fill cut short says "Out of Coins · tank 57 %".
6. **Hospital bays face along the avenue** (Task 1 for the greybox, Task 8 for the city): parked vehicles point along the street in front of the bay, not across it.
7. **Placeholder signs are debug labels.** §7.3 says a placeholder box carries "the id on a sign". A texture per lot for 2,600 lots is a draw-call and memory cost for nothing a player needs, so the id is shown as a label in the map debug view (F4) for lots within 80 m, and every box is coloured by its category.
8. **Steep lanes are diagonal ramps.** A 14% lane across a 14 m band needs 100 m of run, more than the band's 60 m depth, so (as in the prototype) it crosses the band diagonally. The ramp is one rotated box solid; its access zone is a chain of overlapping axis-aligned rects, one per 6 m of run, because `AccessZone` is axis-aligned and the navmesh blockers are boxes anyway.
9. **Switchbacks are a U:** a flat entry stub, a climbing leg along x, a flat hairpin landing, a climbing leg back, a flat exit stub, each leg an x-aligned tilted box at 9%. Inside the U, the ground stays at the lower level.
10. **A 1 m parapet** runs along every upper terrace edge except at connector mouths, so a courier does not run off a 14 m drop by accident (a mantle hops it on purpose; the fall only costs a Fragile bonus).
11. **The player's car model is `mega/Car_01`** (§9 item 3 asks the vehicles sub-project to choose; SP4 used a placeholder). The skateboard, bicycle and motorbike stay code-built placeholders (missing models, §5.5).
12. **Business names come from a word list this plan ships** (`config/names.json`): fictional first words × business kinds × household surnames. shiko reviews the list in the PR for real-brand collisions (§9 item 5).
13. **Navmesh budget:** per-tier recast cell sizes (walk 0.3 m, skateboard 0.4, bicycle 0.4, motorbike 0.5, car 0.6) and gzip on disk and over the wire; `map:check` fails when the five gzipped files total more than `nav.maxNavMeshMB` (10). The SP4 greybox meshes are rebuilt with the same settings.
14. **Offer rolls on the city** (SP4 gap): pickup→dropoff on-foot path distances come from a build-time table (`map/city.distances.bin`), and a roll makes at most `offers.maxPathQueries` (8) runtime queries for the player→pickup leg after a straight-line prefilter.
15. **Parked vehicles are marked on the Map app and the minimap** (playtest "smaller things"). The Garage app's fuel percentages and a Switch action are SP6 work (the Garage and Wallet apps are SP6 per §15).
16. **The project rule "CC0 assets only"** must become "CC0, or the owner's licensed packs listed in `assets/CREDITS.md`" (§16, city design §9 item 1). Only shiko can change the project instructions; the spec's §16 wording is updated in this PR's build.
17. **`front` axis of every building defaults to `+z`.** The packs do not record the door side (§7.2). `pnpm assets:sheet` renders every placed building from its `+z` side so shiko can mark the wrong ones, and the overrides are edited into `map/catalog.json` by hand.

Test in Task 13.
